# 🎵 Megalist of PortMaster Music Creation Projects

> A curated list of music creation software, synthesizers, grooveboxes, trackers, and samplers available on **PortMaster** for Linux handheld devices (Anbernic, TrimUI, Powkiddy, Miyoo, etc.).
---

## 🎛️ LSDJ style trackers

* **[LittleGPTracker (LGPT / Piggy)](https://github.com/djdiskmachine/LittleGPTracker)**  
  *Tracker / Sampler / OG*  
  Samples based workflow. Old and venerable. The OG console tracker.
* **[ChipNomad](https://chipnomad.org/)**  
  *Chiptune tracker*  
  Bit perfect chiptune specialized tracker.
* **[ChooChooTracker](https://choochootracker.pages.dev/)**  
  *Modern Multiengine Tracker*  
  Tracker with a plethora of synths and sound design options.
* **[PocketTracker](https://github.com/conanizer/pockettracker)**  
  *Modern Multiengine Tracker*  
  Tracker with Soundfonts, samples and more.
* **[ElkTracker](https://elktracker.io/)**  
  *Modern Multiengine Tracker*  
  Commercial tracker.  

---

## 💾 Classic syle trackers

* **[SunVox](https://portmaster.games/)**  
  *Modular Synthesizer & Tracker*  
  Modular synthesizer with a tracker.
* **[MilkyTracker](https://milkytracker.org/)**  
  *Classic FastTracker II Emulation*  
  Port of a 30 years old famous desktop tracker. 
* **[Schism Tracker](https://schismtracker.org/)**  
  *Impulse Tracker Re-implementation*  
  Port of another 30 years old famous desktop tracker.

## 🎧 Grooveboxes & Samplers

* **[Pocketacid](https://github.com/boomlinde/pocketacid)**  
  *Mini acid groovebox*  
  Step sequencer groovebox: one drum kit and two acid synths. Joysticks support.

* **[4TRK mini](https://jasonbmusic.itch.io/4trk-mini)**  
  *4 track groovebox*  
  Groovebox with samples and synths.
